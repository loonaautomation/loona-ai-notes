What to write to us about — by tool

MCQ Maker. If a question's options or answer key look wrong, tell us the class, subject and chapter you entered and paste the question that came out wrong (change students' names if any appear — there should be none in an MCQ). Answer-key accuracy is the thing we most want reported, because a wrong key in a printed test is a real classroom problem.

Lesson Plan Maker. For plans that do not match your book or board, include the board, class, subject and the topic exactly as printed in your textbook's contents page. Plans follow the book information you give, so a mismatch usually starts there — and if it does not, we want to know.

Asaan Notes and Short Q&A. If notes come out too hard, too easy or off-topic for the class level, tell us the class and topic and what reading level you needed. "Asaan" (easy) is a promise we calibrate from teachers' reports.

Exam Paper. For paper-pattern questions — marks distribution, section layout, time allowed — say which board's pattern you follow. Patterns differ by board, and a pattern we do not yet cover is a feature request we take seriously.

Anything broken. A tool that produced nothing, a button that did nothing, a result that stopped halfway: tell us the tool's name, what you typed in your own words (never students' personal information), and your phone or computer model. That is enough for us to reproduce most faults.

Feature requests from teachers

Most of what AI Notes does exists because a teacher asked for it. A new tool, a new board pattern, an Urdu-medium option you need — write it plainly, the way you would say it in the staffroom. You do not need technical words; describe the classroom job and we will work out the tool.

Please do not send

Never email us passwords or one-time codes, and never send students' names, photos, results or any child's personal information — every tool here works from the book, class and topic alone, and so does our support.
