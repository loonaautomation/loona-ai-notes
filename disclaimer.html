Loona AI Notes uses artificial intelligence to draft MCQs, lesson plans, easy notes, short questions and answers, and exam papers. AI is a helpful assistant, not an authority, and this disclaimer explains the limits honestly.

AI output can be wrong

Generated material may contain factual errors, invented or missing facts, wrong answer keys, or content that does not match your exact textbook edition. Always verify every result against the official textbook and your own knowledge before using it with students, and never rely on it as the sole source for an exam or an official document.

Not a substitute for professional judgement

These tools support a teacher's work; they do not replace a teacher, a board syllabus, or official board material. Curriculum and examination decisions remain the responsibility of the teacher and the school.

Your input leaves your device

Unlike most Loona tools, which run entirely in your browser, the AI Notes tools send the text you enter to Make.com and Google Gemini so the AI can generate your result, as our Privacy Policy explains.

Please do not enter sensitive personal information into the AI Notes tools — no student names with private details, no passwords, no ID numbers and no private documents. Only enter the class, subject, chapter and lesson content the tool asks for.

Tell us about an error

If a generated result or a page is wrong, please report it via our Feedback page — tell us the tool, the class, subject and chapter, and what was wrong, so we can improve the prompts and checks behind the tools.
