Frequently Asked Questions

Loona AI Notes · Loona Automation

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Honest answers about Loona AI Notes — including exactly what happens to the text you enter.

Is Loona AI Notes free?

Yes. All 5 tools — MCQ Maker, Lesson Plan, Asaan Notes, Short Q&A and Exam Paper — are free to use, with no sign-up.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts on Loona AI Notes. Open a tool, choose your class, subject and chapter, and generate.

How does Loona AI Notes work?

When you use a tool, the text and selections you enter are sent to Make.com, our automation processor, which passes them to Google Gemini, the AI model that generates your result. The result is then shown back to you on this site.

Does my text stay on my device?

No — and we state this honestly because it is different from most Loona tools. The content you enter into the AI Notes tools leaves your device and is sent to Make.com and Google Gemini so the AI can work. Please do not enter sensitive personal information. Our Privacy Policy explains exactly what is sent and why.

Can the AI make mistakes?

Yes. AI can produce wrong facts, wrong answer keys or missing points. Always check the result against your textbook before using it in class or in an exam — the teacher's review is a required step, not an optional one.

Which classes and boards does it cover?

The tools are made for Classes 1–12, for Punjab and Federal board textbooks, as described on our About page.

Who built Loona AI Notes?

Loona AI Notes is part of Loona Automation, built and maintained by Aurang Zeb Loona, a school teacher and AI teacher trainer from Jaranwala, Pakistan.

How do I report a wrong result?

Use the Feedback page or email loonaautomation@gmail.com. Include the tool name, the class, subject and chapter you selected, and what was wrong with the result.
