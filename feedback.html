Feedback & Bug Reports

Loona AI Notes · Loona Automation

Did a tool produce a wrong answer key, a confusing lesson plan, or a result that does not match your textbook? Please tell us — teacher feedback is what improves the prompts and checks behind these tools.

How to reach us

Email us at loonaautomation@gmail.com. There is no form on this page on purpose: a plain email reaches us directly, and you keep a copy of what you sent.

✉ Email your feedback

What to include

· The tool name (MCQ Maker, Lesson Plan, Asaan Notes, Short Q&A or Exam Paper).
· The class, subject and chapter you selected.
· What was wrong — for example, the correct answer from your textbook.
· Your device and browser, if the problem is a page or button issue.

Please do not enter sensitive personal information into the AI Notes tools — no student names with private details, no passwords, no ID numbers and no private documents. Only enter the class, subject, chapter and lesson content the tool asks for.

What happens next

We read every message. Reports about wrong content are checked against the textbook, and the tool's prompts or checks are improved where the report shows a real problem. We cannot promise a personal reply to every message, but every report is used.
