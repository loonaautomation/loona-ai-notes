This page explains honestly how Loona AI Notes works. It is the exception in the Loona network, and we would rather explain that clearly than let you assume otherwise.

Most Loona tools stay on your device — this one cannot

Across most of the Loona network, tools run entirely in your browser and your content never leaves your device. AI Notes is different: artificial intelligence cannot run inside this simple web page, so the tools work through online AI services.

What happens when you press Generate

The text and selections you enter — for example the class, subject, chapter and other choices — are sent to Make.com, our automation processor (EU1, Europe), via the Make (EU1) webhook. Make.com passes that content to Google Gemini, the AI model provider, which generates the result that is returned and shown to you on this site. Our Privacy Policy describes this in the section "AI processing with Make.com and Google Gemini".

Please do not enter sensitive personal information into the AI Notes tools — no student names with private details, no passwords, no ID numbers and no private documents. Only enter the class, subject, chapter and lesson content the tool asks for.

The teacher review step

Every result is a draft. AI can make mistakes, so checking the output against the textbook before classroom use is a built-in step of how we work — please never skip it.

No account, and free

There is no sign-up and nothing to install. The 5 tools are free; the site may show advertising in the future to cover its costs, as our Privacy Policy notes.
